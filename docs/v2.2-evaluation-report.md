# Pre-Ranking v2.2 Evaluation Report

## Purpose

Version 2.2 adds topic-level penalties before any paid AI call. No Telegram,
social-media, video, or other publishing node was executed during evaluation.

## Datasets

- **Validation:** 25 Spiegel and 25 Tagesschau stories. v2.2 was tuned after
  inspecting v2.1 errors on this dataset.
- **Unseen test:** 25 FAZ and 25 DW stories. Labels and v2.2 rules were frozen
  before execution.

## Results

| Dataset | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 | FP | FN | Importance MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Controlled v2.1 | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 0 | 0 | n/a |
| Real-news validation v2.1 | 76% | 75.5% | 100% | 86.0% | 12 | 0 | 1.20 |
| Real-news validation v2.2 | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 0 | 0 | 1.26 |
| Unseen FAZ/DW test v2.2 | 60% | 81.1% | 69.8% | 75.0% | 7 | 13 | 1.70 |

## Interpretation

The perfect validation result does not generalize and indicates overfitting.
The unseen result is the authoritative performance measure for v2.2.

Primary weaknesses:

1. DW is absent from the trusted-source list.
2. Positive vocabulary is too narrow for diplomacy, trade, elections, media
   freedom, environmental policy, and industrial policy.
3. A trusted source can make irrelevant lifestyle or culture stories pass.
4. Title and body evidence have identical weights.
5. Importance is coupled too directly to the relevance score.

## Decision

Do not promote v2.2 to production. Version 2.3 should add source normalization,
title weighting, topic evidence, and separate relevance and importance scores.
A third unseen dataset must remain untouched until v2.3 is frozen.
