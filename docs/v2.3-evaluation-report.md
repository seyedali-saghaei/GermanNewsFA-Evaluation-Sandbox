# Pre-Ranking v2.3 and v2.3.1 Evaluation Report

## Purpose

Version 2.3 replaces broad source-based trust with structured topic evidence,
stronger title weighting, and relevance scoring that is separate from
importance scoring. No paid AI, Telegram, social-media, video, or publishing
node was executed.

## Frozen unseen test

The third unseen dataset contains 50 unique articles from Süddeutsche Zeitung,
ZEIT, and Deutschlandfunk. Labels were assigned before v2.3 was executed, and
the v2.3 rules were frozen before evaluation.

| Version | Dataset status | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 | FP | FN | Importance MAE |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v2.3 | Independent unseen | 88% | 97.1% | 87.2% | 91.9% | 1 | 5 | 0.64 |
| v2.3.1 | Reused tuning set | 98% | 100% | 97.4% | 98.7% | 0 | 1 | 0.44 |

The v2.3 false positive was caused by substring matching: the short token
`eu` matched inside `Leute`. The five false negatives concerned swimming
deaths, fuel prices, malaria, a mercury incident, and natural-disaster risk.

## v2.3.1 changes

1. Short tokens of three characters or fewer now require word boundaries.
2. Public-health evidence covers infections, malaria, swimming deaths, DLRG,
   mercury, and poisoning.
3. Consumer-public-interest evidence covers fuel prices, petrol, diesel,
   filling stations, receipts, rents, and energy prices.
4. Climate/disaster evidence covers natural disasters, the WorldRiskReport,
   floods, earthquakes, extreme weather, wildfires, and drought.
5. Relevance and importance remain separate.

## Interpretation

The 88% v2.3 result is the current independent performance estimate. The 98%
v2.3.1 result is not an independent estimate because the same dataset was used
to design the fixes. It demonstrates that the identified error classes were
addressed, but it does not prove generalization.

## Release gate

Do not promote v2.3.1 solely from the tuning result. Freeze the implementation,
label a fourth dataset before execution, and report that result separately.
